# Findings of the stress round of 2026-11-15

One row for every finding of the eight stress-test reports, numbered `S<report>-<n>` as each report numbers its findings: `S1` check and git, `S2` script rules, `S3` reviewer rules, `S4` dependencies in languages, `S5` the other commands, `S6` the plugin with an agent, `S7` a new user, CI and the local loop, `S8` installation and running. Later findings take `U-<issue>` for a user's report, `R-<n>` for a finding of a review or a hostile test of the build, and `T1` to `T8` for the findings about the tool that Lawcheck's own law raised. The defect and its reproduction are written to stand on their own.

Columns. **severity** is the tester's: `blocks the release`, `should be fixed before` or `after the release`. **layer** is the layer of the test design that shows it (0 harness, 1 contract, 2 hostile inputs, 3 security, 4 limits, 5 environments, 6 real repositories, 7 documentation); `-` where no layer holds a test yet. **package** says who works it: `C` this ledger and its rails, `D` hostile inputs, `E` security, `F` limits, `G` environments, `H` docs and law changes (docs recipes, the law-change notice, the Runes limits stated), `A` the harness, `B` the suite guard, `P` the reviewer providers, `after alpha` where no test of layers 2 to 5 will cover it. **Runes cause** is `yes` where the cause is in the Runes readers. **test** is the test that reproduces it, empty until one exists. **generalised by** is the invariant or property that would have found it. **state** is `open`, `limit` (a limit of Lawcheck itself that is stated and stays), `runes-limit` (a Runes cause, stated as a limit in the docs and the spec, and today's behaviour pinned by a test), `question <n>` (the choice is the owner's and waits in entry n of `spec/questions.md`) or `closed`. A row whose choice the build took as a technical decision stays `open` and names its entry in the defect.

## How a row closes

A row closes only with the name of a test that reproduces the defect and is red before the fix, or with a ruling in `spec/questions.md` that says why the behaviour stays. A row that holds neither stays `open`, `runes-limit` or `question`. The steps for each finding are the ones of the test design: write the reproduction as a test at the lowest layer that shows it, named with the finding id, and see it red; ask which generator or invariant would have produced it and extend that, or add the invariant to the spec; fix, and see the test green; give a guard its sabotage entry. A finding that is as the spec says goes to `spec/questions.md` and its test waits for the ruling. A docs finding is fixed in the page, and the docs layer executes the corrected text. A defect found outside a package's own work is added here with the class, the reproduction and the state `open`, and is not fixed in passing.

A row filled in `test` by a test that only pins today's behaviour (`S5-4`) or by a pending entry of the codes test (`tests/contract/pending-next.ts`) stays open: the test does not yet show a defect that a fix removes.
